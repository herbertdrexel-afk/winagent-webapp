For all users

1 Signing in

  1. Open WinAgent in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari …).
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Click Sign in.

No account yet? Create one via Register – an administrator then approves it. If needed, an administrator resets your password.

2 Overview & menu

The menu is on the left (at the bottom on mobile). These are what you need day to day:

D Dashboard – overview
% Commissions – invoices
R Invoices – turnover
S Statistics – reports
L Suppliers
K Customers

At the top there is a search bar for the open area. On a computer you can keep several areas open as tabs.

The home page is the Dashboard: it shows the supplier statistics as a bar chart (current/prior-year turnover), a pie chart next to it with each supplier's commission share, and the table with all figures below. Use PDF to download the report, or Details → for the full statistics.

Some menu items are only visible to administrators – that is normal.

3 Viewing invoices

Under Invoices you see all turnover (customer invoices).

  1. Choose the supplier at the top.
  2. Choose the period with the buttons: This week, This month, Last month, Q1–Q4, Year – or enter From/To yourself.
  3. If needed, search on the right by invoice no., customer or date.

A click on a row opens the invoice with all line items. While editing, the commission is calculated live (amount × rate); negative amounts appear in red. The total is at the very bottom.

4 Importing data

Turnover does not have to be entered by hand – you can import files.

  1. At the desired supplier, click Upload CSV / Excel.
  2. Select the file. The preview shows all line items.
  3. Check the customer assignment – WinAgent automatically suggests the customer with the highest name match. Addresses that are not found are marked yellow.
  4. Click Import.
No duplicate imports
An invoice that already exists is not created again. Only if the amount or commission has changed is it overwritten – otherwise everything stays unchanged.

Supported formats: CSV, Excel, XML, JSON and DBF. A special format (ND TexPack) is detected automatically.

5 Creating a commission invoice

  1. First choose the supplier and the period as above.
  2. Click Commission statement – WinAgent adds up the commission per currency.
  3. Check the invoice date and choose: Invoice + list (the invoice is saved) or list only.
  4. Click Create – the PDF is downloaded.

The description (“Commission 01-03/26”) is built automatically from the period – for English suppliers as “Commission”, for German ones as “Provision”.

6 Printing & editing invoices

Under Commissions you find all commission invoices created. Filter at the top by supplier and status.

  • PDF AMV – print the invoice in the standard layout.
  • PDF NAAG – the alternative layout, if enabled for the supplier.

Clicking the row opens the edit dialog. There you can change the description, amount, currency, date and status. The changed description is printed on the PDF (AMV and NAAG).

File name: PR-No_Supplier_AMV.pdf or …_NAAG.pdf.

7 Statistics & reports

Under Statistics there are several tabs:

  • Supplier statistics – turnover & commission per supplier, with a prior-year comparison.
  • AdrUms – customers by commission or by turnover.
  • Supplier detail – per supplier the months (Jan–Dec), followed by quarters, half-years and the year.

Set the year or period, click Load, and download with PDF. In the detail PDF each supplier starts on its own page.

8 Suppliers & customers

Suppliers and Customers are the master data. Click a row to edit, or New at the top to add one.

Delete a customer: select the row using the field on the left and click Delete next to “New” at the top. A customer that still has turnover/invoices assigned cannot be deleted – a note appears in that case.

On the supplier you store, among other things, the VAT/UID number, the invoice language and whether “PDF NAAG” is offered.

9 Profile & language

Click your initials (blue circle) at the top right. There you enter your first and last name and switch the language (German / English) – the change takes effect immediately.

For administrators

10 Users & access Admin

Under Users you approve new accounts, create accounts and change passwords.

Per user you define which suppliers they may see (turnover, invoices, reports). With All on/off you quickly select or clear all. Without permission a “403” message appears when opening.

11 Settings Admin

Under Settings you store logos, company data and bank details for the invoice PDFs.

  • Standard (AMV): logo, company data (VAT/UID, registration) and a bank account per currency (EUR/USD/CHF).
  • Bank details NA AG: own logo, issuer, place and bank for the “PDF NAAG” layout. If the fields are empty, the standard bank details are used automatically.

The matching bank account is chosen automatically by the invoice currency – that is why the currency is always stored in uppercase (EUR, USD, CHF).

12 Automatic import & log Admin

Reybex feeds: Store the pull link (with feedToken) per supplier. WinAgent fetches the invoices automatically every day; use Pull now to do it immediately. The same rules as the manual import apply (no duplicate imports).

Log: Here you see every import – automatic and manual – with time, source, file, status and how many invoices were new, updated or unchanged. Entries can be deleted individually or all at once.

13 Good to know

  • Commission (net): the stored rate is already net – sub-agents' shares are deducted.
  • Date & numbers: everywhere in the format D.M.Y and 28,222.20 (German locale: 28.222,20).
  • Currency: always uppercase (EUR/USD/CHF) so bank and reports link correctly.
  • Reports Admin: automatic e-mail reports can be scheduled.

14 Frequently asked questions

I still see old data after a change.
Reload the page: Ctrl + F5 (Mac: Cmd + R).

I get a “403” when opening.
You lack permission for this supplier – an administrator can grant it (section 10).

I can't find “Users” or “Settings”.
These areas are only visible to administrators.

Can I print the handbook?
Yes – from the browser with Ctrl + P. The layout is optimised for printing.
