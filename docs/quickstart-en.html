Quick Start Guide

The most important steps at a glance – explained in a few minutes.

1 Sign in

  1. Open WinAgent in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari …).
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Click Sign in.

No account yet? Create one via Register – an administrator then approves it.

2 Overview

The menu is on the left (at the bottom on mobile). These are what you need day to day:

D Dashboard – overview
R Invoices – turnover
S Statistics – reports
% Commissions – invoices
L Suppliers
K Customers

The Dashboard home page shows the supplier statistics as a bar chart and, next to it, a pie chart with each supplier's commission share.

Some menu items are only visible to administrators – that is normal.

3 View invoices

  1. Click Invoices.
  2. Choose the supplier at the top.
  3. Pick the period using the buttons: This week, This month, Q1–Q4 or Year – or set From/To yourself.
  4. If needed, search on the right by invoice no., customer or date.

The list loads automatically. Click a row to open the invoice with all line items. The total is shown at the bottom.

4 Reports (Statistics)

Under Statistics you can see turnover and commission – per supplier or per customer, compared to the previous year.

  1. Choose a tab at the top (e.g. Supplier Statistics or Customer Detail).
  2. Set the period or year.
  3. Download the report with PDF.

5 Print a commission invoice

Under Commissions you'll find all commission invoices. On the right of each row:

  • PDF AMV – download the invoice as a PDF.
  • PDF NAAG – the alternative layout, if available.

Click a row to open the edit dialog – there you can change the description, and the new text is printed on the PDF too.

At the top you can filter by supplier and status (Open, Paid, Under review).

6 Customers & suppliers

Under Customers or Suppliers: click a row to edit, or New at the top to add one.

Delete a customer: select the row on the left, then click Delete next to “New”. Customers that still have transactions can't be deleted.

7 Language & profile

Click your initials (blue circle) at the top right. There you can set your first name/name and switch the language (German/English) – it takes effect immediately.

Good to know
Still seeing old data after a change? Reload the page with Ctrl + F5. To finish, click the Sign out icon at the top or bottom.
